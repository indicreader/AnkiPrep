Information Collection and Use

AnkiPrep built this app as a Free, Ad-Supported service. This page informs visitors about our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone who uses our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Personal information we request is retained on your device and is not collected by us directly.

The app integrates third-party SDKs that collect, process, and transmit data off-device to identify users, serve advertisements, and send communications. Our third-party service providers include:

Google Play Services Google AdMob Google Play Billing
02

Device Identifiers and Advertising ID (AD_ID)

To support our free tier through advertising, this app explicitly accesses and utilizes the Google Play Services Advertising ID (AD_ID).

Usage

Collected via Google AdMob to deliver personalized ads, manage frequency capping, and combat ad fraud.

User control

Reset or opt out anytime via Settings › Google › Ads on your Android device.

03

Push Notifications and Communication

We may use standard system notifications to send alerts about scheduled review sessions, study streaks, and deck updates.

You can opt out of notification alerts at any time by adjusting notification settings for the AnkiPrep app in your device system settings.

04

In-App Purchases and Billing Data

Our Service offers virtual upgrades and premium features via In-App Purchases processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Payment processing

We do not collect, process, or store financial details, credit card numbers, or bank account information. All transactions are managed by Google Play.

Purchase records

Google Play provides non-sensitive data (order ID, time, SKU) to verify and activate premium features.
