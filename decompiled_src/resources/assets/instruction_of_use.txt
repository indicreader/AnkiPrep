========================================================================
                          INSTRUCTIONS OF USE
========================================================================

Welcome to AnkiPrep! Follow these instructions to set up your flashcards, 
customize your study settings, and maximize your learning efficiency.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INGESTING YOUR STUDY MATERIAL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
AnkiPrep supports two primary ingestion methods:

A) CSV Imports:
   - Prepare a CSV file where the first column is the question/front of the 
     card, and the second column is the answer/back of the card.
   - Example row format: "What is photosynthesis?","Process of converting light to chemical energy."
   - Go to the deck selection dashboard, select "Import CSV", and select 
     your file. AnkiPrep automatically generates MCQ options by drawing 
     semantically matching distractors from other cards in the same dataset.

B) APKG (Anki Package) Imports:
   - Export your deck from Anki or AnkiWeb as a `.apkg` package.
   - Select "Import APKG" on the dashboard. AnkiPrep will unpack and load 
     the cards into your local offline database recursively, preserving 
     your deck hierarchy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CHOOSING YOUR STUDY MODE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Go to Settings to select your preferred study mode:

- Practice Mode:
  - Instant feedback after every answer.
  - Displays detailed explanations and allows reviewing the correct choice 
    immediately to reinforce memory.

- Test Mode:
  - Strict exam simulator. No immediate feedback.
  - Answers are submitted silently, and a comprehensive scorecard is 
    displayed only at the end of the session.

- Revision Mode:
  - Focuses strictly on your weak areas.
  - Leverages FSRS memory algorithms to schedule cards you previously answered 
    incorrectly, ensuring focused reinforcement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CUSTOMIZING THE STUDY INTERFACE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adjust the App settings to fit your preferences:
- Dark Mode: Toggle between Light Mode and the premium Dark Mode 
  (Obsidian Bookshelf theme, utilizing obsidian base and soft indigo accents).
- Font Style: Choose from Default, Serif, Sans-Serif, or Monospace typography.
- Question Limit: Restrict sessions to 5, 10, 20, 50, or all available cards.
- Time Limit: Add a countdown timer (15s, 30s, 60s, or unlimited) per question.
- Vibration Feedback: Toggle haptic feedback on/off for answers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATABASE & DATA MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sync Now: Updates your active decks with any changes and re-builds 
  semantic relationships.
- Clear Cache: Deletes cached layouts and resets study progress if you 
  want to start fresh.
- Note: Since AnkiPrep is local-first, clearing all application data from 
  Android system settings will wipe the local database. Keep your source 
  CSV/APKG files backed up.
========================================================================
