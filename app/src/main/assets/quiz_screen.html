Which of the following describes the mechanism of action of SSRIs in the treatment of depressive disorders?

Figure 1.2: Serotonin Synaptic Gap
check_circle Explanation

The correct answer is A. SSRIs primarily work by inhibiting the serotonin transporter (SERT) at the presynaptic membrane, leading to an increased concentration of serotonin in the synaptic cleft. This is fundamental to their antidepressant effect.
