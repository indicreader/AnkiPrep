Welcome to AnkiPrep!
------------------------------------------------------------------------
AnkiPrep is a premium, offline-first Android application designed to 
revolutionize how you prepare for exams, learn languages, and retain 
complex information. Utilizing state-of-the-art learning science, 
AnkiPrep converts standard flashcards into highly engaging multiple-choice 
questions (MCQs) utilizing advanced distractor-generation algorithms 
and Spaced Repetition (FSRS scheduling).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
About Vijay Prakash
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vijay Prakash is an independent software engineer and technology creator

Believing that effective learning tools should be fast, offline-first, 
and accessible to everyone, Vijay developed AnkiPrep to bridge the gap 
between passive reading and active recall.

Our mission is to help students, developers, medical professionals, 
and lifelong learners optimize their study time through optimized cognitive 
science principles.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core Principles of AnkiPrep
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cognitive Science: Leveraging active recall and spacing effects to 
   double retention.
2. Offline Privacy: Your data belongs to you. All import pipelines and 
   learning state databases run entirely on your local device.
3. Premium Aesthetics: Beautiful design options including the signature 
   Obsidian Bookshelf theme, dynamic styling, and haptic feedback.
